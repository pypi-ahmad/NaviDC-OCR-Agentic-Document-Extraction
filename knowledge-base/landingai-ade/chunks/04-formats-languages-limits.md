# ADE formats, languages, and limits

Documented v1 inputs: PDF, common and API-only images, DOC/DOCX/ODT, PPT/PPTX, CSV, XLSX. Office files convert to PDF and layout may change. API CSV/XLSX limit is 50 MB. XLSX processes up to 65,536 rows and columns; excess is omitted.

Sync Parse accepts 100 PDF pages. Parse Jobs accept 1 GB or 6,000 pages. Rate limits are plan-level, distributed per minute, and shared across an organization.

Strong support is listed for English, Chinese, Dutch, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, and Spanish. Fourteen languages are moderate and several complex scripts basic. DPT-3 Verity is Latin-script only. Validate business documents directly.
